# SFX brief: <VIDEO TITLE> short

You're helping Michael Chan source sound effects from **Artlist** (artlist.io) for a <LENGTH>-second vertical
video with hand-drawn, ink-on-paper motion graphics (stamps, marker scribbles, pop-ins, a light switch,
falling coins, checklists). Michael is signed in to Artlist in Chrome. The audio goes under spoken
dialogue, so sounds should be short, clean and punchy, not cinematic or reverb-heavy.

## Rules

- **Ask Michael before every download.** For each sound, show him the shortlist (title, creator,
  duration, link) and your pick, and wait for a yes. Batching several sounds into one approval is fine
  if he agrees to that.
- Don't change Chrome, Artlist account or system settings, and don't enter any passwords. If you're
  signed out, stop and ask Michael to sign in.
- Download from Artlist's own SFX library only, under Michael's subscription.
- Prefer **WAV**, otherwise the highest-quality format offered.

## Where to put the files

Save everything to `<PROJECT>/sfx/`, renamed to the name in the first column (keep
the original extension), e.g. `stamp.wav`. If you take a second option, add `-2` (`stamp-2.wav`).

Also write `<PROJECT>/sfx/manifest.csv` with these columns:
`file,artlist_title,creator,artlist_url,duration_s,notes`
